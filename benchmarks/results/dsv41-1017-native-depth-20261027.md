divided by the server's request-decode duration. It excludes prefill/TTFT and
is neither client aggregate throughput nor SSE-chunk counting.

## Vision, tools and control validation

Target-only and the original K5 service each passed 12/12 initial canaries:
both aliases, arithmetic, continuous streaming, tool calling, high reasoning,
five synthetic vision fixtures including a two-image case, and two
image-swaps. The hook-enabled candidate also passed 12/12 before the sweep.
These establish image dependence on these fixtures, not broad vision quality.

Both ranks logged every fixed-depth policy change. Native depth-file reloads
are synchronized across TP ranks every 64 records; 512 output-token warmups
cover a reload even at the full K5 acceptance length.

An earlier harness incorrectly required `draft_tokens / drafts == fixed_depth`.
The exact pinned scheduler counts `len(scheduled_spec_token_ids)`; the GPU
runner separately masks expert rows and rejects out-of-depth drafts. Thus
that quotient measures scheduled proposals, **not selected GPU depth**. The
faulty assertion triggered an unnecessary restore/reload despite both ranks
logging `fixed:3`. It was corrected before the completed sweep; no failed
validation was silently treated as a pass.
