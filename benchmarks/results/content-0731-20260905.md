## Relationship to Anemll's published GB10 results

The [Anemll reference](https://github.com/Anemll/dspark-vllm-gx10#performance) publishes best aggregate rates of 48.5/70.4/103.5 tok/s at C1/C2/C4 for its 512-token explanation workload. This suite preserves that explanation prompt but reports medians on 0731, with explicit seed/warmup/metric handling. The published checkpoint/statistic differs; do not calculate an A/B speedup from those numbers. Client SSE decode proxies and server decode timing are also distinct metrics.
